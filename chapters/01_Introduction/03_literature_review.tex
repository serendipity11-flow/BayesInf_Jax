\section{Literature Review} 
\label{sec:introduction:literature_review}
 This section reviews the relevant literature in four aspects: applications of deep probabilistic models for Bayesian inference; state-of-the-art probabilistic approaches for system identification and state estimation; and data-driven control methods based on deep probabilistic models.
\subsection{Deep Probabilistic Models for Bayesian Inference}  
\paragraph{Deep Gaussian Processes}
\acfp{DGP} are sequential compositions of Gaussian processes, where the outputs of one Gaussian process are the inputs to the next. They extend the single-layer \ac{GPR} and can represent compositional or hierarchical structure of variables. A survey \citep{jakkala2021survey} summarizes the improved methods of \acp{DGP}. \citeauthor{abraham2023deepgp} \citeyearpar{abraham2023deepgp} proposed a \ac{DGP}-based method for Bayesian inference of compositional parameters from \acp{PDE}. The parameters are assigned with a \ac{DGP} prior which models compositional functions. Then, the posterior distribution of the parameters is optimized via variational inference.
Applications of \acp{DGP} for Bayesian inference span multiple domains. For instance, \citeauthor{dgp2018park} \citeyearpar{dgp2018park} utilized \acp{DGP} to identify contaminant release sources, while \citeauthor{dgp2023desai} \citeyearpar{dgp2023desai} employed them for air quality estimation. In biomedical contexts, \citeauthor{NIPS2017_861dc9bd} \citeyearpar{NIPS2017_861dc9bd} leveraged \acp{DGP} to assess patient survival under competing risks. These studies show promising results in comparison to \acp{GPR} as well as other machine learning methods, and highlight the versatility and effectiveness of \acp{DGP} in addressing parameter inference challenges. However, the computational cost and storage cost remain the main drawbacks of \acp{DGP}, especially for high-dimensional data.

\paragraph{Bayesian Neural Networks}
Applying \acfp{BNN} for Bayesian inference is usually combined with other methods for better extracting information from observation data. For example, \citeauthor{YANG2021109913} \citeyearpar{YANG2021109913} integrated a \ac{BNN} with \acfp{PINN} (B-PINN) to infer parameters in \acfp{PDE} with Gaussian noise. \acp{PINN} construct a neural network to approximate the forward solution to the underlying \ac{ODE} or \ac{PDE} problem by minimizing reconstruction loss of data, while regularized by the underlying governing \ac{ODE} or \ac{PDE} residual loss. Meanwhile, model parameters can also be estimated probabilistically via \ac{MCMC} or variational inference. The B-PINN achieved more accurate results with stronger noise in observations. citeauthor{merkatas2022bnn} \citeyearpar{merkatas2022bnn} combined a \ac{BNN} with a autoregressive model to identify stochastic system dynamics. citeauthor{yan2024brnn} \citeyearpar{yan2024brnn} proposed a Bayesian \ac{RNN} as a Bayesian filter for state estimation of non-Markov dynamical systems. This method outperformed other time-series modeling methods including \acp{RNN} and Transformers. \acp{BNN} are flexible to be integrated with other models and can provide uncertainty quantification, at the cost of higher computational complexity. On the other hand, applying \acp{BNN} for Bayesian inference is not straightforward and must be combined with other architectures. Besides, the uncertainty of network weights does not necessarily translate to the uncertainty of the target variables.

\paragraph{Conditional Variational Autoencoders}
\acfp{CVAE} represent a conditional extension of the \acf{VAE} framework. \acp{CVAE} have been widely adopted for amortized Bayesian inference across various scientific domains. For example, \citeauthor{vae_sbi} \citeyearpar{vae_sbi} introduced a \ac{CVAE}-based approach for efficient Bayesian inference in scientific simulators.
In astronomy, \acp{CVAE} have enabled Bayesian parameter estimation from gravitational wave data, as demonstrated by \citeauthor{Yamamoto_2020rse} \citeyearpar{Yamamoto_2020rse}, \citeauthor{Gabbard2022} \citeyearpar{Gabbard2022}, and \citeauthor{Sun_2025} \citeyearpar{Sun_2025}. Additionally, \citeauthor{Ferreira_2025} \citeyearpar{Ferreira_2025} applied \acp{CVAE} to infer the equation of state of neutron stars. In biomedical engineering, \acp{CVAE} have been used for neuroimaging analysis under varying correlated parameters \citeyearpar{cvae2025}, and for parameter estimation in mouse cardiac muscle studies \citeyearpar{TUNE2025179}. These examples highlight the versatility and effectiveness of \acp{CVAE} for Bayesian inference of high-dimensional data. One shortcoming exhibited in the research \citep{Sun_2025} is that the \ac{CVAE} tends to estimate broader posterior distributions compared to other methods, indicating a potential limitation in capturing precise uncertainty.

\paragraph{Conditional Normalizing Flows}
BayesFlow (in Section \ref{sec:condnf}) \citep{radev2020bayesflow} provides a general framework for amortized Bayesian inference using conditional \acp{NF}. Besides our work, it has also been applied to various scientific domains. For instance, \citeauthor{Kucharsk2025} \citeyearpar{Kucharsk2025} applied BayesFlow in multiple cognitive models. \citeauthor{Zeng2023} \citeyearpar{Zeng2023} demonstrated its effectiveness in damage detection in structural health monitoring. \citeauthor{Rahmandad2025_uh} \citeyearpar{Rahmandad2025_uh} utilized BayesFlow to infer parameters from system dynamics, such as random walk model. Some other works share similar idea with BayesFlow. \citeauthor{De_Santi_2024} \citeyearpar{De_Santi_2024} referred the summary network as an embedding neural network. \citeauthor{zhdanov2022gisaxs} \citeyearpar{zhdanov2022gisaxs} extended the condition with richer synthetic data, which is generated by a \ac{CVAE}. \citeauthor{DONG2025117457} \citeyearpar{DONG2025117457} arranged a conditional \ac{NF} through taking the conditioning variable as an additional input to each coupling layer and enlarging the latent space as well. These works demonstrate the effectiveness of conditional \acp{NF} for amortized Bayesian inference. Especially, the design of the summary network needs to be tailored to the specific characteristics of the data, which is crucial for extracting informative representations from observations and inferring parameters precisely.

\paragraph{Diffusion Models}
Diffusion models have recently emerged as powerful tools for Bayesian inference due to their ability to model complex, multimodal distributions. \citeauthor{zhu2025diffusion} \citeyearpar{zhu2025diffusion} introduced a diffusion-based framework for parameter identification in dynamic power systems, demonstrating improved accuracy over traditional methods. Conditional diffusion models have also been applied to infer parameters, unobservable states, and system dynamics in \acp{PDE} \citep{haitsiukevich2024}. Furthermore, \citeauthor{baldassari2023} \citeyearpar{baldassari2023} developed a framework for conditional score-based diffusion models in infinite-dimensional function spaces to perform Bayesian inference for linear inverse problems. \citeauthor{chen2025conddiff} \citeyearpar{chen2025conddiff} proposed a conditional diffusion model for amortized posterior estimation, showing superior performance compared to conditional \acp{NF}, particularly in reconstructing multimodal distributions and capturing variable boundaries. Nevertheless, the computational cost of inference with diffusion models is much higher than other \acp{DGM} due to its iterative sampling procedure.
