\section{Outline} 
\label{sec:introduction:outline}

The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chapter \ref{chap:methods} outlines the methodologies developed and employed throughout this work. These methods are grouped into three categories: parameter identification, state estimation, and optimal control.
    \item Chapter \ref{chap:experiments} presents the experimental validation of the proposed methods, structured according to the aforementioned categories.
    \item Chapter \ref{chap:conclusion} summarizes the key findings of the dissertation and proposes directions for future research.
\end{itemize}
