## CONTRIBUTING: AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions are permitted only when the contributor has reviewed and understood the complete contribution, verified its correctness and security, and has sufficient rights to submit it under this project's license. Material AI assistance must be disclosed in the pull-request description. Public-code matches, third-party content, and new dependencies must be identified and reviewed. The human contributor remains responsible for the contribution. Maintainers may reject unreviewed, unverifiable, low-quality, or predominantly autonomous submissions.

Do not use AI tools to remove or obscure copyright, license, NOTICE, attribution, or provenance information. Do not submit confidential information, credentials, personal data, or material that you are not authorized to disclose.

## README legal-status note

State the license accurately and link to `LICENSE`. If third-party components have separate terms, point to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`. Do not call a repository open source before an applicable license is granted. Clearly label experimental, unmaintained, unsupported, research-only, or non-production status without adding use restrictions that conflict with the selected open-source license.
